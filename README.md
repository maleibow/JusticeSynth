# JUSTICE SYNTH

A live-performance Web Audio synthesizer and visual telemetry instrument driven by U.S. incarceration spending, private prison equities, custody mortality records, NYC daily detention rosters, and prisoner civil-rights filings.

## Key Features

* **Procedural Gaussian-Splat Fluid Stage & 3 View Modes (`1` / `2` / `3` / `V`):**
  * **`CLOCK + FLUID` (`1`, default):** Monumental live-ticking telemetry clock across the top interacting directly via gravity droplets with the fluid energy vortices below.
  * **`CONSOLE` (`2`):** Full 5-channel data-weight mixer console alongside the fluid stage and live clock.
  * **`NO TEXT` (`3` / `V`):** Removes all text, headers, cards, and node labels to leave pure fluid dynamics, opposing water currents, dark shear rifts, directional money-into-stock / people-siphon streams, custody death shockwaves, and permanent memorial sky stars.
* **Bare-Bones Data Inspector & Weight Sculpting:** Click any live clock readout, channel card, `INFO` button, or fluid energy circle to inspect the exact source, what it indicates, update cadence, `Last updated` timestamp, un-truncated records, and per-stream `FORCE / DATA WEIGHT IN SYSTEM`.
* **Full Key & Mode Support:** Transpose the entire instrument across all 12 root keys (`C`–`B`), 9 musical modes (`Dorian`, `Natural Minor`, `Phrygian`, `Harmonic Minor`, `Major`, `Lydian`, `Mixolydian`, `Minor Pentatonic`, `Open Fifths`), and `440 Hz` / `432 Hz` reference tuning for live sets with other instruments.
* **Hybrid Synth Engine Backend (`B`):** Vital/Serum-style wavetable choir, Dexed-style 4-Operator FM metallic cluster, 3-band sculptural noise, concrete cell comb resonator, and custom `AudioWorklet` wavefolder/bitcrusher protected by a `-2.0 dBFS` brickwall limiter.

## Live Telemetry Sources

1. **Private Prison Stock (`NYSE: CXW`, `NYSE: GEO`):** Intraday equity quotes / market close tape (`SEC CIK 0001070985` / `0000923796`).
2. **Federal Carceral Spending (`USAspending.gov API v2`):** DOJ Bureau of Prisons & DHS ICE contract awards plus continuous session expenditure (`$2,560.55/sec` direct, `$5,771.18/sec` total burden).
3. **Custody Mortality (`UCLA Law Behind Bars` & `Incarceration Transparency`):** Individual BOP, ICE detention, and jail mortality records.
4. **Courts & Families (`Federal Register BOP API`, `CourtListener`, `Vera / PPI`):** Live Bureau of Prisons notices, prisoner civil-rights filings, and family/pre-trial metrics.
5. **NYC Daily Inmates In Custody (`NYC OpenData 7479-ugqb`):** Live daily NYC Department of Correction census, pre-trial detention (`DE`/`DEP`/`DPV`) & Brad H. mental health observation shares, and anonymized individual custody records.

## Live Shortcuts

* **`1` / `2` / `3` / `V`**: Switch View Modes (`CLOCK + FLUID`, `CONSOLE`, `NO TEXT`)
* **`Space` / `M`**: Mute / Unmute Audio
* **`D`**: Pulse Custody Death Record
* **`C`**: Pulse Federal Contract Award
* **`S`**: Latch Human Force Balance
* **`B`**: Toggle Synth Engine Backend Drawer
* **`F`**: Toggle Fullscreen Stage
* **`Esc`**: Close Data Inspector
